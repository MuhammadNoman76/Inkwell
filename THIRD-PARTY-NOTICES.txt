Inkwell - third-party software notices
======================================

Inkwell bundles open-licensed fonts, uses Microsoft's Fluent UI System Icons, reads and writes Word
documents (.docx) with the Open XML SDK, imports Markdown with Markdig and writes PDF files with
PDFsharp. Their licences are below. Inkwell is an independent project, not affiliated with or
endorsed by Microsoft or any of the projects below. Microsoft Word and Microsoft Office are
